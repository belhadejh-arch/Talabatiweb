---
name: Real-order driver dispatch
description: Production driver pushes must originate from public-menu orders and be claimed once in PostgreSQL
---

Only orders created by the public customer flow (`ANDROID_CUSTOMER`) are eligible for driver email dispatch. Assignment rows must persist driver sequencing and a one-time notification claim/result; each operation carries the exact order_id, assignment_id, and driver_id triple. Driver-facing invoices and emails include committed order details and delivery location/maps only for DELIVERY orders; restaurant invoices must not expose customer location or contact details. Startup scans must not redispatch arbitrary historical orders. An untouched PENDING delivery with no send attempt may be delivered after Gmail is connected, but old FAILED or ambiguous SENDING deliveries require human review. If no external request began because credentials or subscription eligibility are missing, preserve a safe retry; never reset SENDING after a request begins. The driver workspace is invoice-only: show real customer invoices for the assigned driver even when email delivery is pending or failed, and keep accept/reject actions in the email.

**Why:** Broad scans and replaying failed/ambiguous mail can produce duplicate or stale notifications; conversely, genuinely queued orders with no send attempt should not be stranded solely because Gmail was connected later. The user wants invoice visibility in the driver workspace independent of email success, location available to drivers for delivery, restaurant privacy, and actionable accept/reject decisions in email.

**How to apply:** Preserve the public-order source marker, current-order/driver validation, exact assignment identity, and database notification status whenever changing assignment, retry, timeout, manual-dispatch, or driver-notification code. Do not gate the driver's invoice feed on email delivery status; keep the feed limited to real, assigned, non-cancelled customer orders and keep accept/reject in email, not dashboard controls. Do not replay legacy orders or retry an old FAILED/ambiguous send blindly. Imported databases must create foundational restaurant/driver tables before additive runtime-schema checks.
